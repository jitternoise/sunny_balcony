Paint tools

Click a cell to apply the selected tool; clicking a cell that already holds that terrain removes it. Painting terrain on a blocked cell unblocks it first.
Pool creates a whole 4-hex lake (anchor + 3 cells, rhombus where it fits); click the anchor to remove the lake, Lake cell adds/removes a single cell of an adjacent lake. Hydro places a 3-cell plant (centre + left + right). Preset bakes the chosen block into the level; a preset Wall covers its anchor and the cell to its right.

Controls

• Click/tap a cell to paint the selected tool. Click-drag pans, the wheel zooms, ⤢ fits.
• Coordinates are axial (q, r) for both grid styles; water falls in +r.
• The playtest tab runs the real beat cycle one measure at a time: PLACEMENT → WATER → TERRAIN → STATUS.

Rules ported from HexBoard.gd

• Pointy grid: natural fall alternates down-left (−1,+1) / down-right (0,+1), first step down-left; a blocked or solid target makes it try the other diagonal; both blocked → it waits. Flat grid: a "straight" stream tries (0,+1) only; a "zigzag" stream alternates (−1,+1)/(+1,0).
• Solid to natural fall: Wall, Catapult, undug dirt, an inactive Hydro Plant, an activated geyser. Diverters and Splitters are pass-throughs.
• The Wall is 2 tiles wide (anchor + (1,0)); when that does not fit the engine mirrors it to anchor + (−1,0); when neither fits the tap is a no-op. Presets never mirror.
• A lake (4 cells) needs 4 beats of contact; all cells share one counter, one beat per beat. A geyser wakes after 3 contacts and becomes a source. A Hydro Plant is solid until water touches it and the player activates it; then its 3 cells become sources.
• Water pressing on dirt for 10 beats triggers a mudslide that collapses up to 3 dirt tiles.
• Town cell or past the lowest playable row = loss. Win = every fire out and every lake full.
• A level with no fires and no pools wins on measure 1.

File format

Fields are written in the repo's usual order: level_id, display_name, intro_text, grid_radius, corridor_half_width, grid_style, source_flow_style, water_sources, fire_cells, pool_targets, lake_cells, preset_blocks, starting_inventory, blocked_cells, hint_cells, town_cells, geyser_cells, dirt_cells, total_block_budget, hydro_plant_cells, par_measures. Optional fields are omitted when empty/zero unless the imported file already had them. Array fields go on one line; dictionaries one entry per line, as Godot writes them.
