Controls

• Click/tap a cell to paint the selected tool.
• Click-drag (past a small threshold) pans the view instead of painting — same tap-vs-drag distinction Level.gd uses for scrolling vs placing blocks.
• Mouse wheel zooms toward the cursor. Use ⤢ to fit the whole board back on screen.

Rules this tool enforces (ported from HexBoard.gd)

• Water's natural fall zigzags down-left/down-right, alternating per stream, first step always down-left.
• Only a Wall blocks natural fall (retries the other diagonal, or pools in place if both are blocked). Diverters/Splitter are pass-throughs — water lands on them fine, then that block's behavior decides the next step.
• A pool needs 4 connected beats total (cumulative, not reset by a disconnect). A pool consumes every stream that reaches it forever, so it must be the last stop on a branch.
• A town cell or the bottom edge is an instant loss.
• Gotcha: a level with zero fire cells and zero pool cells wins vacuously on tick 1 — always add at least one of each unless that's intentional.
• Design convention: keep the playable width at or under 9 tiles (grid_radius ≤ 4 for a full diamond, or use the corridor generator for a narrower band on a taller grid_radius).
